A little about me

I’m a high school student interested in the parts of computing you don’t usually see: how processors execute instructions, how operating systems manage hardware, and how languages get compiled.

Most of my projects start with a question. Building something is how I try to answer it.

Away from the keyboard, I’ve spent years in competitive robotics and ran a small PC business on the side. I like the mix of software, hardware, and figuring things out as I go.
